## 2024-10-18 - Added Accessibility Labels to Custom Interactive Elements\n**Learning:** Custom interactive components built with `Pressable` in this app (like `KeyStampGrid`, `StudioFader`, and various steppers) often lacked `accessibilityRole` and `accessibilityLabel` attributes, which are essential for VoiceOver/TalkBack users to understand their function.\n**Action:** Always ensure that any `<Pressable>` component used as a button or toggle includes `accessibilityRole="button"`, a descriptive `accessibilityLabel`, and `accessibilityState` (if applicable) to clearly communicate its purpose and state to screen readers.
